DraftCraft

Script Writing in a way you know. Open source and free peer-to-peer.

DraftCraft is a screenwriting app for your desktop (Linux, Mac and Windows). You write in the familiar screenplay format, and your scripts open and save as Final Draft (.fdx) files, so you can still swap work with people who use Final Draft.

What it does

  • Writing: standard screenplay formatting, revisions, version history, and spell check in several languages.
  • Character sheets: profiles with photos, backstory, and other details.
  • Import and export: Final Draft, Fountain, Fade In, Open Screenplay Format, Word, and PDF.
  • Working together: live co-editing with other writers, directly between computers, with no company server in the middle.
