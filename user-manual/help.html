Help and Feedback

Found a problem, or have an idea?

DraftCraft is free and open source under the MIT licence. Problems and ideas are welcome on the project page:

  • Report a problem or suggest something
  • Download the latest version

When you report a problem, it helps to say which system you use (Linux, Mac or Windows), the DraftCraft version shown in Help, About, and what you were doing when it happened.
