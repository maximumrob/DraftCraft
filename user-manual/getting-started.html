Getting Started

Your first launch, and how to open a script.

The Welcome screen

When you open DraftCraft, a Welcome screen appears. It offers a short sample script so you can try the editor before you write anything of your own.

Once you know your way around, tick Hide Welcome Screen on subsequent launches and it will stay out of the way. To bring it back, open Settings and turn on Show the Welcome screen when DraftCraft starts.

Opening and saving

Use the File menu to open a script or to save the one you are working on. DraftCraft opens Final Draft (.fdx) files directly, along with its own .dcraft files and the other formats listed on the Saving and File Formats page.
